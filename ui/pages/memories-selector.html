Step 1: Request Your Memories

Follow this guide to make your request via the Snapchat app

Steps to follow:

  1. Open the Snapchat app and click on the profile icon at the top left
  2. Click on the gear icon (settings) at the top right
  3. Scroll down and click on "My Privacy & Data"
  4. Click on "My Data"
  5. You will be redirected to the browser to continue - then proceed to the next step of the guide
Shortcut: Go directly to the website

If you prefer, you can go directly to https://accounts.snapchat.com/v2/download-my-data. Log in to your account and then proceed to the next step.

Automatic Redirection

After clicking on "My Data", you will be automatically redirected to the Snapchat website in your browser.

Step 2: Select the Data to Export

On the Snapchat website, choose what data you want to export

What to check:

  1. Check "Export my memories" - this contains all your photos and videos
  2. Check "Export JSON files" - required for SnapLoad to read metadata
  3. Uncheck all other options - you don't need chat history, etc.
  4. Click the "Follow" button on the Snapchat page
Why JSON format?

It contains download links and metadata. SnapLoad cannot work without it.

Step 3: Choose the Period

Select the period of your Memories to download

Steps to follow:

  1. Select the period you want to download from the dropdown menu
  2. To retrieve everything, choose the "All Time" option
  3. Click on "Next"
  4. Continue to the next step
All Your Memories

Select "All Time" to get all your photos.

Step 4: Wait

Wait time depends on data volume

Quick Case

Minutes to 1 hour

Little data

Normal Case

A few hours

Most users

Long Case

Days

Many Memories

Reference:

1500 Memories takes approx 20 minutes.

What happens now?

  1. Snapchat is preparing your data - just wait
  2. You'll receive an email from Snapchat when ready
  3. Proceed to step 5 upon receipt
You can close the app

Close SnapLoad and return once you have the email.

Check spam!

Snapchat's email often arrives in spam.

Step 5: Download Your Data

To do on your computer - Retrieve the file

Use your computer

You must use your PC to access the link and download the file.

Steps to follow:

  1. You've received the email from Snapchat
  2. Open the link in the email on your PC
  3. Log in to Snapchat
  4. Click "View Exports"
  5. Click "Download"
  6. A ZIP file will download
  7. Proceed to the next step to import
ZIP File Downloaded

The ZIP contains your data. You'll import it next.

Automatic Import

  1. You've downloaded the ZIP (mydata~XXXX.zip)
  2. SnapLoad searches Downloads folder
  3. Uses most recent file if multiple found
  4. Extracts memories_history.json automatically
  5. Imports without manual action

Advantage: Fast and automated.

Manual Import

  1. Click button to select file
  2. Choose JSON or ZIP file
  3. Confirm selection
  4. Import starts

Advantage: Good for specific file selection.
